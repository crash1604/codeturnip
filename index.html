System design that clicks

codeTurnip publishes short, animated explainers that make system design concepts easy to see: latency, scaling, reliability, queues, and the trade-offs behind real architectures.

New episodes appear on weekdays on Instagram and TikTok as @codeTurnip. The first series, System Design Foundations, covers the vocabulary every backend engineer uses, from requirements and capacity estimates to percentiles, SLOs, and bottlenecks.

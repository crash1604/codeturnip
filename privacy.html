Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information codeTurnip handles through this website and through codeTurnip Publisher (“the Publisher”), the internal tool codeTurnip uses to publish its own videos to its own social media accounts. “codeTurnip”, “we”, and “us” refer to the operator of the codeTurnip channel.

Who uses the Publisher

The Publisher is used only by codeTurnip, to post codeTurnip’s own videos to accounts that codeTurnip owns or manages. It has no public sign-up, and it is not offered to other people or businesses. The only social media accounts ever connected to it are codeTurnip’s own.

Information the Publisher accesses

TikTok

Through TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API, the Publisher accesses the connected TikTok account’s basic profile information (such as its open ID) and permission to upload videos to that account (user.info.basic, video.upload, and, if enabled, video.publish). It uses this only to upload codeTurnip’s videos and to check whether each upload succeeded. It does not read the account’s videos, followers, messages, comments, or analytics.

Instagram and Facebook

Through the Instagram API with Facebook Login, the Publisher accesses the IDs and names of the Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account that codeTurnip manages, and permission to publish Reels to that Instagram account (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish). It uses this only to publish codeTurnip’s videos with their captions and to confirm that each post succeeded.

Google Drive

The Publisher stores finished codeTurnip videos in codeTurnip’s own Google Drive and shares each video by link so Instagram can retrieve it for publishing. It does not access other files.

How information is stored

  • Access tokens stay on codeTurnip’s own computer. TikTok tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Instagram, Facebook, and Google credentials are kept in the encrypted credential store of a self-hosted automation tool that only that computer can reach.
  • The Publisher keeps a local publishing log with each video’s status and the post or upload IDs returned by Instagram and TikTok.
  • codeTurnip does not copy this information to any other server or service.

How information is used and shared

Information accessed by the Publisher is used only to publish codeTurnip’s videos and to record the result. codeTurnip does not sell, rent, or share it, and does not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose. Videos and captions are sent to TikTok, Instagram, and Google only as needed to publish or store them, under those companies’ own privacy policies.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. codeTurnip does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or forms on it. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to serve the pages, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while the Publisher needs them and stop working when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app’s security settings, under the permissions granted to apps and services.
  • Facebook and Instagram: in Facebook settings, under Business integrations.
  • Google: in your Google Account, under third-party connections.

When access is revoked, codeTurnip deletes the related tokens from its computer. To ask about or request deletion of any information described here, email chanakyadevpro@gmail.com.

Children

The Publisher is not used by anyone other than codeTurnip, and codeTurnip does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
